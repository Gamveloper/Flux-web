FLUX · Quick start

Build a world in about 30 seconds:

  1. Play: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump. On mobile, use the joystick and JUMP button.
  2. Build: choose Build, pick a brick size and color, then click/tap the world to place bricks.
  3. Select: select a brick to move, resize, rotate, or change its material. Use Undo if you make a mistake.
  4. Explore: open FLUX Tools for vehicles, logic, effects, secrets, and replay features.
  5. Keep it: use Save .FLUX to export your world. Multiplayer lets friends join with a room code.
